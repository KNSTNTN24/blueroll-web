Quick one about page 3 of your pack.
A fridge log only protects you if every line shows three things: a temperature, a time, and initials. "OK" in the box is what inspectors call a dead record. "3.2°C, 07:41, AB" is evidence.
Two tricks from kitchens that pass:
1. Tape page 3 to the fridge door, not in the folder. The fridge nobody signs is always the one furthest from the pass.
2. Missed a check? Don't backfill it. Write "missed, re-checked 14:00". An honest gap reads better to an EHO than a suspiciously perfect month.
