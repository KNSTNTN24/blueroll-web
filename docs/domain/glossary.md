> What each word means in Blueroll, what the web app, the mobile app and the database call it, and where to read more.
> Product terms first, then the food-safety and regulatory vocabulary behind them. Names in `code` are DB columns, tables
> or constants. Links are relative to `docs/domain/`.

### Words with more than one meaning

Say which one you mean: **team** (members vs WhatsApp team) · **group** (multi-site business vs Telegram group vs capability group) ·
**sign-off** (completion vs Plan) · **draft** (checklist vs setup review vs AI text vs unsigned Plan) · **section** (SFBB vs menu) ·
**override** (capability vs pack vs dietary) · **source** (completion vs pack vs payment vs allergen) · **code** (invite vs Telegram link) ·
**token** (6-digit invite vs 32-hex form vs Plan share vs store purchase) · **method** (26 pack safe methods vs 9 recipe HACCP methods vs 6 own-words methods) ·
**library** (17 setup checklists vs the list of all templates) · **review** (4-weekly review vs setup Review tab vs "Review" = awaiting sign-off) ·
**manager** (the Manager tier vs "owner or manager" in every `isManager`/`is_business_manager` check vs `sites.manager_id`) · **flag** (out-of-range answer vs "Flag an issue" = report an incident).

## Food-safety and regulatory terms

Page numbers are from the FSA *Safer food, better business for caterers* pack as chunked in `supabase/functions/_shared/knowledge-sfbb.ts`
(© Crown copyright, OGL v3.0). Figures marked *not in the pack text* come from `knowledge.ts` curated notes or app copy and should be re-sourced before being presented as SFBB.

| Term | Means | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **HACCP** | Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point: the law requires every food business to have written food-safety procedures based on it. SFBB is the FSA's jargon-free way of meeting that duty; Blueroll's "HACCP Pack/Plan" are SFBB-shaped, not a classical HACCP study. | SFBB p.2 |
| **CCP** | Critical control point — a step where control is essential (e.g. cooking core temperature). The term does not appear in SFBB or in Blueroll's UI; checklist temperature items with ranges play that role. | SFBB p.2 (concept) |
| **FSA** | Food Standards Agency, the UK regulator that publishes SFBB and runs the hygiene-rating scheme. Blueroll reads its establishment API at onboarding. | `copy.ts` OGL line; [Business](entities/business.md) |
| **SFBB** | *Safer food, better business*: the FSA pack for small caterers — safe methods to complete before trading, plus a diary. Blueroll's pack mirrors its five sections. | SFBB p.2–6 |
| **Safe method** | One SFBB sheet: a *Safety point*, *Why?*, and *How do you do this?* for the business to fill in; reviewed and updated when operations change. | SFBB p.4 |
| **Check it / Think twice! / What to do if things go wrong** | Recurring SFBB sub-headings: how to verify a method, exceptions, and the corrective action plus how to stop it recurring. | e.g. SFBB p.41, p.60–61 |
| **Prove it** | The SFBB management sheet on using a disinfected probe to show a method works, and the "Prove it: records" diary pages. Blueroll's Plan uses the same label for the records linked under each method. | SFBB p.68–69, p.93 |
| **Cross-contamination** | Harmful bacteria or allergens spread onto food from other food, surfaces, hands or equipment; SFBB section 1 (personal hygiene, cloths, separating foods, food hypersensitivity, physical/chemical contamination, pests, maintenance). | SFBB p.12–26 |
| **Cleaning** | SFBB section 2: handwashing, cleaning effectively (clean, then disinfect with a BS EN 1276/13697 product), clear and clean as you go, cleaning schedule. | SFBB p.28–37 |
| **Chilling** | SFBB section 3: chilled storage and display, chilling down hot food, defrosting, freezing. | SFBB p.39–47 |
| **Cooking** | SFBB section 4: cooking safely, foods that need extra care, reheating, acrylamide, checking your menu, hot holding, ready-to-eat food. | SFBB p.49–63 |
| **Management** | SFBB section 5: opening and closing checks, extra checks, Prove it, allergen information, training and supervision, customers, suppliers and contractors, stock control, product recall. | SFBB p.65–78 |

| **Chilled storage** | Chilled food kept at **8 °C or below** — a legal requirement in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, recommended in Scotland; fridges recommended set at **5 °C or below**. | SFBB p.41, p.68 |
| **4-hour rule** | Chilled food may be displayed out of refrigeration once, for up to four hours; after that throw it away. | SFBB p.41 |
| **Chilling down** | Cool cooked food "as quickly as possible" then refrigerate; divide, shallow trays, ice bath, blast chiller. The "within 90 minutes" in app copy and library checklists is *not in the pack text*. | SFBB p.42–43 |
| **Freezing / defrosting** | Frozen food kept at **−18 °C or below** and frozen before its use-by date; defrost in the fridge, microwave or cold running water, fully, and do not refreeze unless cooked. | SFBB p.44–47, p.68 |
| **Cooking safely** | Cook until steaming hot throughout; no pink meat, clear juices. Safe probe combinations: **75 °C for 30 s**, 70 °C for 2 min, 80 °C for 6 s, 65 °C for 10 min, 60 °C for 45 min. ("75 °C for 2 s" in some app copy is wrong, #101.) | SFBB p.50–51 |
| **Extra-care foods** | Eggs, rice, pulses, shellfish, and any process outside the pack (sous vide, curing, rare burgers…) that must be shown safe to the local council. Blueroll's `extra_care_flags`: `eggs`, `rice`, `pulses`, `shellfish`. | SFBB p.52–53 |
| **Reheating** | Reheat to a safe centre temperature, only once; check several spots in microwaved food. "82 °C in Scotland" (Scottish regulations) is *not in the pack text*. | SFBB p.54–55 |
| **Hot holding** | Hot food kept at **63 °C or above**; may be displayed below that once for up to **two hours**, then reheated once or chilled, else thrown away. | SFBB p.60–61 |
| **Ready-to-eat food** | Food that will not be cooked again (salads, cooked meats, sandwiches): keep apart from raw food and chilled. | SFBB p.62–63 |
| **Fitness to work** | Food handlers with vomiting or diarrhoea must not handle food until 48 hours after symptoms stop. | SFBB p.13–14 |
| **Probe check** | A digital probe should read −1 to 1 °C in iced water and 99 to 101 °C in boiling water, checked e.g. monthly; disinfect before and after use. | SFBB p.69 |
| **14 allergens** | The regulated allergens customers must be told about: celery, cereals containing gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, lupin, milk, molluscs, mustard, peanuts, sesame, soybeans, sulphites, tree nuts. Blueroll keys: `EU_ALLERGENS` in `src/lib/constants.ts`. | SFBB p.19–20, p.70–72 |
| **Food hypersensitivity** | SFBB's umbrella term for allergy, intolerance and coeliac disease; the "Food allergies" safe method. | SFBB p.19–20 |
| **PPDS / Natasha's Law** | Since October 2021, prepacked-for-direct-sale food must carry a full ingredients list with allergens emphasised. | **"Free from"** | A claim that must be backed by preventing any contact with that allergen; Blueroll derives Gluten-Free/Dairy-Free from the *absence* of a tag, which is weaker (#84). | SFBB p.71 |
