Safe method 3.1 · Chilling
Chilled storage
Safety point
Chilled food is kept at 8 °C or below; we aim for 0–5 °C.
Why?
Harmful bacteria multiply fastest between 8 °C and 63 °C. Cold food stays safe until its use-by date.
